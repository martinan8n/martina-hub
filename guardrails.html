Spend limits, approval gates, and running/awaiting states are settled patterns already, but scattered across the Goal-based tasks explorations. This collects the finished ones in one place. Nothing here is new work — these screens still live in Goal-based tasks too, this is a duplicate, not a move.

🧩 Problem

Usage/limits settings, the nav-level running/awaiting indicators, and the cost-cap-reached gate were each designed and settled inside Goal-based tasks V2, but they apply anywhere an agent can run, spend, or need approval — not just long-running goal-based sessions. Buried inside one workstream, they're easy to miss when reviewing guardrail behavior generally.

🧪 Hypothesis

Collecting the settled guardrail patterns — final Settings design, nav Running & Awaiting states, and the real-AIA-grounded Cost cap gate — into their own workstream makes them easier to reference and keep consistent everywhere an agent's spend or approval status shows up, without re-deciding anything already decided.
